Hi!

My name is *Nora*, and my pronouns are *she/they*. This is my blog, ***Ivory Tower***.

This blog was, in large part, created out of my growing qualms with social media (X and Instagram in particular) and the way we have discussions around the things we care about. Far too often, I'd find posts or comment sections filled with people being confidently wrong or blatantly disingenuous about all sorts of topics. I'd always resist the urge to reply, though. I knew, deep down, that doing such a thing would be pointless. After all, when's the last time you saw someone get their mind changed in a reply section, no matter how well thought-out or sound their argument was? I'm willing to wager your answer is "never." I know I haven't ever seen it.

And, like, obviously! No surprises there. Maybe, once upon a time, people would have been more willing to have real discussions on the internet, in the era of IRC and chatrooms. But I come to you from late 2026, and its so rare to find critical thinking out in the wild.

I'm hesitant to use the word "stupid" (or it's relatives). Yes, stupid people exist. But I think it's an overgeneralization to say that the reason for this is that the human race is getting stupider. Rather, we're are being conditioned to refuse critical thinking by technology, and the capitalist hand that guides it. Companies like Meta care about user engagement, *not* user well-being.&#x20;

On sites like X, you absolutely *can* try to have intellectual discussions with people you disagree with, hear them out, or even try to convince them! But you'd be wasting your brainpower. Social media platforms have conditioned users to reply quickly and carelessly. It's simultaneously easier and more likely to get good metrics on your post if you make a quippy reply rather than a well thought-out argument (see [The Alt-Right Playbook: Never Play Defense](https://youtu.be/wmVkJvieaOA?si=SSh7pqtTL1EVSNdH\&t=129)). I could go on and on about this (maybe I will, in a [non-existent future post](/about/).)

But I want to clarify: I'm not trying to solve this problem. That would be impossible. It's systemic, and it's a symptom of the greater techno-capitalist ecosystem that we have to have these discussions in.&#x20;

**My purpose for this blog is twofold:**

1. **Open an avenue for me to share my thoughts in a longer format.** Social media nearly requires users to share posts quickly. Sometimes, it's a hard limit, like X's staggeringly small post character limit (unless you pay!). Other times, it's a softer limit, enforced by the platform's design and your audience's attention span. I want to be able to share my thoughts completely and entirely, without worrying about feeling like I need to prune my thoughts to keep it concise.
2. **Provide a place for real discussion.** I do this through the [Ivory Tower Discord server, which doesn't exist yet](/about/). More on this later.

And admittedly, an important third reason:

3. **It's fun.** Like, really fun. I hope the webpage design and my writing shows how much I enjoy things like this. This site will never run ads, and never will be to make money. I'm just passionate, and happy to be here.
